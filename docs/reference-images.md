# Reference images

These photos and drawings were used only as modelling references. They are not in this repository, and none ship in the page. This list records where each came from, its licence and how it was scaled.

These files are modelling references. All were opened and checked by eye before being kept. Only files with an open licence below may ship in the page, and those need the credit shown. Files marked "All rights reserved" stay out of the page.

Commons files were fetched on 1 Nov 2026. The front, rear, three-quarter and engine bay photos are Commons' 1920 or 1280 px versions because the originals were rate limited. The five side views are the originals.

## Side elevations and scale

Wheelbase is 2588 mm on every X100 (Jaguar: 101.9 in). Scale = 2588 / distance between the wheel centres in pixels. Coordinates are x, y in pixels from the top left of the local file, read off a grid on zoomed crops of the hub caps. Expect about 2 px of error on the small files and about 5 px on the large one.

| File | Size | Car faces | Front wheel centre | Rear wheel centre | Distance px | mm per px |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| side-coupe-xk8-40-early-pure-elevation.jpg | 5137 x 3136 | right | 4212, 1967 | 1382, 1950 | 2830 | 0.914 |
| side-coupe-xkr-facelift-long-lens.jpg | 1024 x 685 | left | 274, 530 | 683, 532 | 409 | 6.33 |
| side-coupe-xkr-2002-tight-crop.jpg | 1494 x 516 | left | 300, 359 | 1154, 359 | 854 | 3.03 |
| side-convertible-xk8-42-roof-up.jpg | 1470 x 522 | left | 277, 377 | 1129, 380 | 852 | 3.04 |
| side-convertible-xk8-42-roof-down.jpg | 1476 x 489 | left | 287, 355 | 1139, 365 | 852 | 3.04 |

Use `side-coupe-xk8-40-early-pure-elevation.jpg` as the master. It has the most pixels and the longest lens.

The scale holds only in the plane of the wheels. The nose and tail curve away from the camera, so they read short. On the master the measured length is about 1.78 wheelbases where the real car is 1.84. Stretch the overhangs to the real 4760 mm length and do not take them straight off the photo. The tight crops are closer to the car and read shorter still. They are good for section lines and shut lines, not overall length.

The two convertible files show the same late XK8 4.2. Wheel sizes differ between these photos, so scale from wheel centres and never from tyre size.
